What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization: making sure AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google’s AI Overviews name your business and cite your pages when people ask about your category. It’s sometimes called AEO. Ranking measures it by asking the engines your prompts and keeping the answers.

How do I get recommended by ChatGPT?

Three steps. Let AI crawlers in and make your answers readable without JavaScript. Get listed where AI looks: business profiles, review sites, Wikidata and the pages that compare your category. Then measure by asking ChatGPT your buyers’ questions and tracking whether you’re named. Ranking has a tool for each step.

How soon will I see results?

You see where you stand as soon as the first checks run. Being named takes longer: AI engines pick up new listings, reviews and pages over weeks rather than days, and nobody can honestly promise when. Track weekly and watch the rate and its interval.

Can I see traffic and sign-ups from ChatGPT?

Yes, if you connect Google Analytics 4. Ranking checks daily for visits and sign-ups from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Copilot, and emails you only when there are some. Included for everyone; one click turns it off.

How do you measure AI visibility?

For each prompt and engine we store the full answer, the brands it names in order, and the sources it cites. Visibility is the share of answers that name you, shown with a 95% Wilson confidence interval.

Do I need an llms.txt file?

We don’t score it. In our own tests AI crawlers almost never fetched llms.txt. Crawler access in robots.txt and answers visible without JavaScript matter far more, and the site audit checks both.
