Billing

Billing questions.

Anything else? Contact us →

Do I need a plan?

No. Pay per check from $1 gives you on-demand checks, with no automatic monitoring. Starter ($39 a month) and Growth ($119 a month) add automatic weekly monitoring on all six engines; Growth adds client reports and the API and MCP. Pay yearly and get 2 months free.

Plan or pay per check?

Paying per check is on-demand, so a small site pays a few dollars a month for what it runs, but nothing is monitored automatically. If you want the same questions checked every week with alerts, a plan is simpler: one price, all six engines, no credit to watch.

What does a plan cover?

The weekly AI checks for your tracked prompts, in all six engines, up to your plan’s limit: 40 prompts and 1 brand on Starter, 125 prompts and 3 brands on Growth. Google rank checks, keyword research, competitor and backlink lookups are pay as you go from credit on every plan.

What happens when credit runs out?

Pay-as-you-go checks pause and we tell you; scheduled ones wait until you top up. Credit is never topped up from your card automatically. On a plan, the weekly AI checks for your tracked prompts keep running.

Does credit expire?

No. Unused credit stays on your account until you use it.

Can I switch or cancel a plan?

Yes, any time in Billing. Canceling stops the renewal, and the plan stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Plan fees are non-refundable, including the unused part of the period.

Can I get a refund?

Credit is non-refundable, but it never expires, so you can start with $1 and top up only what you use. For Set it up for me, the $299 setup fee is non-refundable, and you can cancel the monthly minimum any time from the billing page.
